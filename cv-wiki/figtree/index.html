11/2023 – 07/2025 • Sydney, Australia

With 40+ years in the market, it supports 150+ organisations globally across four countries, delivering cloud-based SaaS solutions to insurers, government and enterprise customers.

Versatile role covering test automation, QA and manual testing activities (40/20/40 split). Gained broader experience with software development tools, including Atlassian Jira, Confluence and GitLab. Played a key role in regression testing: planning, executing, and reporting test outcomes and defects. Contributed to strengthening the team’s quality practices by promoting root cause analysis (RCA), rewriting acceptance criteria, and leading workshops on exploratory testing, test coverage and effective bug reporting. Helped establish a unit testing framework using Vitest and TypeScript, leveraging experience with AI tools and access to GitHub Copilot. Actively supported team upskilling through practical examples, coaching and toolkits.
